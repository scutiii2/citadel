# Bruiser: visual identity

## Established design

Bruiser is a hulking, hunched basalt creature with a distinctly boar-like head, blunt snout, pointed ears, two heavy warm-ivory tusks and furious glowing amber eyes. It has a broad torso, oversized fists and short, sturdy legs. It must read as a creature, with no human face.

Its dark brown-grey stone hide has molten amber cracks. Both fists wear rusted iron bands. One shoulder carries a large spiked iron pauldron; the other remains exposed stone. A cracked belly plate contains the brightest molten glow. Sparse embers rise from the shoulders. No separate weapon.

Palette: basalt brown-grey, slate iron, rust orange, warm ivory, amber and yellow-orange. Chunky square pixel clusters, hard edges and no smooth gradients. Its own molten core supplies the strongest light.

The single pauldron is on the creature's left shoulder (viewer right in the frontal reference), with rust-orange edging, rivets and pale slate spikes. Wrist bands are broad slate iron cuffs with rust-orange rims. Maintain that anatomical shoulder across poses.

## Consistency

Keep the head, snout, tusks, ears, torso-to-limb proportions, fist bands, single pauldron, belly plate and molten-crack pattern consistent across all sprites. Only pose, framing and backdrop change. Battle shading must stay at or above dark slate (approximately #2a3342), including outlines.

Main: full body, both fists raised overhead, facing the viewer, within the central safe band of a dark forge scene. Preview: head, massive shoulders and one fist, simplified dark amber halo. Battle: full body facing right in a heavy wind-up, one fist drawn back and the other held low; transparent background, no ground or shadow.
