Move brains to Stronghold (Plan 5.2b)

Stronghold keeps the encrypted Obsidian brain connections, Note
publication and AI promotion suggestions in Treasury. Stronghold has no
sessions, so it asks Portcullis what an account may do on every request
(new /internal/accounts/{uid}/permissions); a permission taken away
applies at once. Armory resolves connections through Stronghold's
POST /brains/resolve (STRONGHOLD_URL replaces EMBER_API_URL). Portcullis
/api/brains routes are pass-throughs; auto-save asks Stronghold whether a
destination is writable. Account delete purges brains through Stronghold.

No data migration: connections, promotions and suggestions start empty,
so saved Obsidian API keys must be entered again.

1310 passed.
