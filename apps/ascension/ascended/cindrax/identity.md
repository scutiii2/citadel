# Cindrax visual identity

Primary reference: `sprite_preview_card.webp`. Use this established portrait as the exact design reference for main card and battle recreations. `../ART_GUIDE.md` controls style and framing; `art_prompts.md` controls poses.

- A floating magma crystal fiend, with a broad armoured upper body narrowing into a pointed, segmented lava-and-obsidian lower body. No walking legs or boots: preserve the hovering tapered silhouette.
- Jagged charcoal/slate obsidian plates over a bright molten orange-red body; cracks and gaps expose lava. Bronze/gold edging, straps and round rivets.
- Angular orange-red mask-like face with two fierce glowing yellow eyes, a pointed chin and no human facial features.
- Tall central crimson crystal crown surrounded by jagged obsidian helmet spikes; shorter red side spikes.
- Huge asymmetrical faceted red/orange crystal spikes fan upward behind both shoulders like rigid wings. Keep their prominent scale and angular shape.
- Heavy spiked shoulder plates, bronze-trimmed chest armour enclosing a large vertical diamond-shaped red crystal in a dark diamond setting.
- Thick armoured forearms and open molten claw hands with long hooked talons, dark knuckle armour and lava seams.
- Layered pointed lower-body armour with bronze waist edging and a glowing molten tip. Small floating crystal/obsidian fragments may accompany it.
- Palette: charcoal/slate, bronze/gold, crimson and lava orange; yellow eyes. Its own molten glow lights nearby armour.

Preserve face, crystal crown and wings, chest gem, claw construction, hovering anatomy, colours and proportions across sprites. Only pose, framing and background change. Battle faces right; raise the darkest character tones to dark slate (about #2a3342 or lighter), including outlines and recesses, for the arena's lighten blend.
