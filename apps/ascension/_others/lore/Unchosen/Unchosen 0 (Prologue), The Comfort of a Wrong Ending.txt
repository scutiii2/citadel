# The Comfort of a Wrong Ending

---

## I. The Tale of the Lost Soul (a fragment)

There was once a story about a man who loved a girl he could never have.

People told it in different ways.

Some called it a story about obsession. Others called it grief, or loneliness, or the plain foolishness of holding on to something that was never his to hold.

Some said the creature in the tale was only a shape given to his refusal to let go — a monster grown from love that had nowhere left to go.

The story was beautiful, told that way.

It was also wrong.

No one who repeated it had ever asked the one question that mattered: whether the curse had been real, or whether he had simply believed in it long enough to make it true.
