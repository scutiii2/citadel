# Twoychi: art prompts

Rules: `../ART_GUIDE.md` (read it first; it explains sizes, the black background and the crop steps).
Files for this Ascended: `sprite_main_card.webp`, `sprite_preview_card.webp`, `sprite_battle.webp` (this folder).
Status: no sprites yet. Design brief: `identity.md`. Once the first portrait is approved, save it as `sprite_preview_card.webp` and name it the primary reference in `identity.md`.

## How to run it

1. Read `identity.md`. Start a new chat. Paste the main card prompt and iterate until Aya, Troy and his two forms are right.
2. In the same chat paste the preview prompt, then the battle prompt, each starting with: "Use the character in the previous image as the exact reference: same design, colours and proportions. "
3. Crop and convert with the commands in `../ART_GUIDE.md` section 6 and check section 7.

## Main card prompt (`sprite_main_card.webp`)

```text
Create the card illustration of an original game creature. Twoychi is one game creature made of two beings, Aya and Troy, always shown together. Aya is a calm, kind young woman who looks entirely human, the way ordinary people see her: plain travelling clothes with a mended sleeve, always in blue and white (a white top or tunic with blue panels, trim and sash, blue-and-white cloth throughout), simple boots, loose dark brown hair, a calm human face with pale cyan-grey eyes. She is the focal figure in front. Behind her, her true form is revealed because she is using her power: a larger, pale, softly luminous water-bender spirit, partly translucent, in cyan and white, with water showing through it. The spirit's hands are translucent like a ghost's, glass-clear white with a cyan glow at the palms, and water gathers in and flows from them as cyan-and-white ribbons, arcs and orbs. Its hair drifts as if underwater in cyan-to-white strands and its eyes are very pale cyan-white. It mirrors her pose, a little larger than her, and fades to mist toward the edges so it never hides her. Troy is her protector dog, a white-and-black shih tzu: small, sturdy, long-coated, with a short flat face, round dark eyes, a dark nose and a feathered tail curled over his back, in white and black patches. Troy also has two larger forms, a wolf and a bear. Each form has the true face and body of its animal (a wolf's long muzzle and pointed ears, a bear's broad head and round ears), not a shih tzu face; what stays the same is the white-and-black colour pattern: the same white-and-black patch layout, the same dark mask and white markings around the eyes and muzzle, and the same plume of white on the tail or rump, so it reads as Troy. Pose: human Aya stands in the centre with both hands open and water flowing between them; her true spirit form rises large and translucent right behind her, mirroring her pose with its own translucent hands pouring water; Troy the small shih tzu sits close in front of her, calm; ghostly and half-transparent at the two sides, the wolf form and the bear form rise, so all three forms of Troy read in one image. Water curls around everyone, tying cyan and white to Troy's black and white. Format: landscape 3:2 (1536 x 1024). Composition: the whole group visible and centred, filling about 85 percent of the height of the middle band, where the middle band is the middle 66 percent of the image height. Keep the top 17 percent and the bottom 17 percent as plain background only, with nothing important there, because the picture will be cropped to a wide 1.93:1 strip. No limb, water stream or effect touches the left or right edge. Background: a dark ruined shrine or dungeon with a faint distant glow, soft and darker toward the edges so the characters stand out, never brighter than the characters. Style: Chunky high-resolution pixel art in a dark-fantasy style: visible square pixels about 4 to 6 real pixels wide, crisp hard edges, no smooth gradients, no anti-aliasing blur, not painterly, not 3D, not photographic. Palette: cyan and white lead (water, spirit glow, Aya), with white-and-black fur on Troy, a little cool slate, and the shared ember-orange only as faint distant forge glow in the background. One strong light source that comes from the character's own cyan glow. A clear silhouette, calm and a little fierce. Avoid pure white and pure black. Never: No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, signature, speech bubbles, UI, card frame or border. No other characters besides Aya (human form and true form behind her, one being) and Troy (they are one Ascended). No ordinary humans, no blood.
```
