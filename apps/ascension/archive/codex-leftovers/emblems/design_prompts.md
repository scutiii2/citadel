# Ascension EMBLEM tier designs

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Six standalone pixel-art capture seals, sharing a hexagonal forged-metal housing and a diamond energy core. Concept artwork only; not wired into the UI.

Tier order and colors follow `configs/ascension_catalog.json` and `web/src/components/theme.css`. EMBLEMs represent capture strength, not elemental types. Distinct ornament silhouettes reinforce the colors.

| Tier | File | Identity |
|---|---|---|
| Common | common.png | Silver core, simple iron rim and copper clasps |
| Rare | rare.png | Emerald core and angular side guards |
| Unique | unique.png | Sapphire core, layered bands and compass points |
| Royal | royal.png | Amethyst core, crown crest and swept side guards |
| Legendary | legendary.png | Gold core, radiant points and upward wings |
| Forbidden | forbidden.png | Crimson core, horned broken metal and red fissures |
