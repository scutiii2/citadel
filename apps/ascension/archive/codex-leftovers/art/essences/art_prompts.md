# Essence material designs

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-13. Transparent PNG art concepts; not yet wired into the game UI.

Essence belongs to a tier and is shared across Ascended. Star upgrades cost one spare copy plus 1 Common, 2 Rare, 3 Unique, or 4 Royal essence respectively. Legendary and Forbidden currently have no star advancement; their designs complete the essence family.

The icons follow the game's pixel-art direction and tier colors. Shapes distinguish tiers without relying only on color.
