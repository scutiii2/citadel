# Ascension lore

Source stories for Ascension. Five short novels share one world. This file is the working index: what is in `lore/`, the Ascension types the lore defines, and every character we could turn into an Ascended.

Everything below comes from the `.txt` files (the PDFs are the same text per novel). Where the text is silent, this file says so rather than inventing. Items marked **inferred** are our reading, not stated outright.

## How to use this file

- Picking the next Ascended: go to [Characters per novel](#characters-per-novel), choose someone with a clear power and look, then check [Ascension types](#ascension-types) for the type.
- Drawing an Ascended: read its `ascended/<id>/identity.md` first (see [Visual identity files](#visual-identity-files)), then `ascended/ART_GUIDE.md`.
- Keeping canon straight: see [Continuity](#continuity-between-novels) for who is the same person across novels.

## The world in short

1. **Scapur.** The Creator-figure (no gender, no body) made worlds, then humans on Scapur. Humans chose violence. He took a human body and lived among them for centuries, finally as **Grin**, ruler during the Intergalactic War.
2. **Grin and Hail.** Grin loved a woman, Hail. They had five children, each carrying a fragment of him: the first **Ascendants**. Hail died. A mob came for Grin; he sent the five children away in pods. Missiles killed two. One pod was crippled and drifted. Two crashed on a world later called Earth.
3. **Earth.** The two crashed Ascendants sleep underground; their power became the **Deities**. Humans, exiled to Earth, were helped by Deities. Their children were **Hybrids**, who split into two peoples: **Enchants** (raised among Deities) and the **Evolved** (raised among humans). The third child arrived last, was worshipped, and made the **Divine** (see Mei).
4. **Hexon and Ascension.** The Evolved found Hexon ore and made *Pure Ascension*. That opened more paths (see types below).
5. **Out-of-Time.** Separate, wider multiverse: a Council outside time, time-strings, Blips, and Havoc, who broke it into many dimension-strings.
6. **Street level.** The Revolver (a team of eight codenamed killers), the Collector (the immortal cursed man, also Ghost and the Illusionist), and the Undeserving island (the origin of Twoychi: Aya and Troy).

## Ascension types

The lore names **six** types of Ascendant. In the game every Ascended currently has `"ascension_types": ["enchant"]` in its `catalog.json`, so the other five are unused so far.

| Type | What it is (from the text) | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pure** | Strengthens the bond an Ascendant already has with their source. Done with Pure Hexon, or rarely by a *Paradox* with no Hexon. Climbs in steps, with no fixed threshold. Costs: it drains crops, animals, nearby people. | Ascendants 2 |
| **Divine** | Serves the Creator, not a world. Made by Mei's ritual: the person survives the altar and is reshaped. Heavenly Power. Those who refuse to surrender it are stripped and cast down to be ordinary. | Ascendants 2, 4 |
| **Abyss** | Named in the list of six and **never explained** anywhere in the lore. | Ascendants 2 |
| **Enchant** | Draws on the living world (forests, tides, a planet's core). The stronger the bond, the more they hold. A dying world diminishes the Enchant. | Ascendants 2 |
| **Synthetic** | Replaced organs, then whole nervous systems, with silicon and alloy; minds can reach machine networks and back themselves up. Also: APSC's trial-made Synthetic Ascendants, who gain new abilities from a serum. | Ascendants 2, Out-of-Time 3 |
| **Crimson** | A body that died of Ascension and rose again. Healed by spent blood, stronger with every act of violence, hungrier as it grows. Crimson Hexon and Corruption Parasites belong here. | Ascendants 2 |

Other terms that are *not* types: **Evolved**, **Enchants** (as a people), **Deities**, **Hybrids**, **Paradox**, **Ascended** (a person who has Ascended; also the game's word for a collectible).

Open decisions for us:
- What is **Abyss**? The text says only that six paths exist, then hints a seventh may ("nothing in those records explained why the number should stop there").
- Which of the existing seven Ascended map to which type? None is mapped yet.
- Lore characters are people. Game Ascended are creatures (fox, golem, crystal fiend). Decide whether an Ascended is the person, a manifestation of their power, or a creature of the same type.

## Continuity between novels

- **Ghost = the Collector = the Illusionist.** Revolver 8 ends with Ghost surviving the fall as "The Collector." Unchosen 1 gives the same name set. The mask, codenames and "trained a single recruit" match Dagger.
- **Maze** sold out Ghost in Revolver and the Collector's list in Unchosen: the same betrayal told twice. Treat as one person.
- **Twoychi = Aya + Troy.** Born in Undeserving 7; the woman the Collector loves in Unchosen; the medium of death in Undeserving 1. Aya is the merciful, calm one and can revive the dead whose fate is not done; Troy stops the dead from fleeing and changes personality with his form.
- **Telepathy**: Out-of-Time's APSC founder and Unchosen's "Telepathy" illusion (a fake of a dead powerful sentient) share a name. The text never says they are the same person. Out-of-Time hints his borrowed face is not what it seems.
- **Twilight** is not a child of Grin. Undeserving hands the story from Twoychi to her.
- **The two dead children of Grin are Chrono and the Reflection.** Chrono survived the energy sync and became Time; the Reflection did not and was sealed in the Mirror Dimension. Unchosen may never say so outright.
- **Grin and his children**: Mei is the third child; the storyteller in Ascendants 5 is Grin (inferred). Two children died in the sky, which fits Chrono and the Reflection; the two sleeping on Earth made the Deities.
- **The immortal man** is Ghost, the Collector and the Illusionist (confirmed). Undeserving 1 shows Twoychi meeting him again and again and failing to finish his death.
- **Name for Earth's peoples**: Deities, Enchants, Evolved, Hybrids appear in Ascendants and Out-of-Time; the Revolver, Unchosen and Undeserving never name a type.

## Story decisions (from your planning chats)

Sources: your five shared planning chats (`0e6d136a…` Twoychi; `1d1a7c20…` Unchosen; `559bd728…` Out-of-Time; `bc547319…` The Revolver; `f81f60a3…` The Creator).

- **Cycle title: Unchosen.** Narrator is "He Who Knows", third-person omniscient. Chapter files: Prologue (the Tale of the Lost Soul, first part), 1 The Collector, 2 Twoychi, 3 The Collector and Twoychi, 4 The Reflection, 5 The Reflection: New Body, 6 the Tale's epilogue. The `Unchosen` folder matches: files 0 to 6 with chapter titles.
- **The curse is his belief.** He remembers it as "everyone feels disgust", but the text never confirms it. The witch may be the shape his own wound took. People can care for him; they just do not love him as he needs. He never resolves it.
- **Ghost came first.** Order of his life: Ghost in the Revolver era (trains Dagger, builds codenames so identity can't be used against him; Maze betrays him), then the Collector identity, then Aya, her gentle refusal, the Mirror Dimension, the Reflection. In Unchosen, "Ghost" is a return to an old habit, not an invention.
- **Dagger.** He shaped Dagger deliberately and enjoyed it because it was the one bond that could not leave. The reveal sits late in Unchosen, inside the Reflection's merged mind, right after the Aya material.
- **Twoychi.** Aya died before she met the Collector and revived as the death spirit. In Unchosen she must look like a normal girl, with only shadow beats (a soldier who should have died and doesn't; a too-long look at him; kneeling over his wound faster than possible; almost staying). The full mechanism lives only in the standalone piece "The rest she cannot take" (Undeserving).
- **Cross-series lore stays implicit.** Unchosen may not state the Grin or Chrono link. Cross-readers recognise it, others feel only mood.
- **Ending.** No narrator conclusion. Two juxtaposed images: Aya's kept carved scrap of wood (love real) and the last empty mirror still waiting (curse real).
- **Telepathy**: Unchosen's dead powerful sentient (whose face the Collector's illusion wears) is read as Out-of-Time's APSC founder; Out-of-Time gives him the earlier life.
- **He Who Knows.** The narrator of all five novels is Grin after he forgot himself (The Creator, part 5). Narration is third-person omniscient, shows and never concludes.
- **Names of the series.** "The Creator" is the working title of the Ascendants novel. The Revolver, Out-of-Time, Unchosen and Undeserving keep their names.
- **Revolver structure.** Eight parts for eight bullets: Part 1 (Ghost's disappearance), Parts 2 to 7 (Virtuoso, Euphoria, Swift, Deadeye, Tech, Disposer), Part 8 (Dagger). Maze has no part of his own; his motive stays in fragments. Each part: a present-day frame, a non-chronological backstory, one deniable Maze trace, one unresolved private fear.
- **Revolver ending (planned).** Eight chambers, so Dagger fills one of two empty ones: Ghost's, or Maze's (killed by Ghost). Or he is the revolver itself, made by Ghost and taught by all seven. The ending never says which. Maze is "he" (confirmed). The revised ending is now in `Revolver 8, Collateral.txt`; `Revolver.pdf` still has the old ending.
- **Out-of-Time structure.** The Council, Telekinesis, Telepathy, the Council part 2, then Chrono. The last beat is separate from the main plot: if one dead child of Grin became Time, what became of the other. Chrono is that child.
- **Collector stays apart from the Council.** He is never named, flagged or investigated by APSC or the Council. His mark is curatorial (missing records, objects that vanish and return altered), never temporal. Reign and Telepathy meet him in another series, alive or dead.
- **Uniz** (Out-of-Time part 2 hinge): the rogue analyst keeps checking a file the Council closed. Not resolved.

## Adding or changing lore

1. Put new novels in their own folder here, parts as separate `.txt` files.
2. Add a row to the folder table and a section under "Characters per novel".
3. Only record what the text says. Mark inferences as **inferred**.
4. If a character becomes an Ascended, add the lore link to `identity.md` and the type to `catalog.json`.
