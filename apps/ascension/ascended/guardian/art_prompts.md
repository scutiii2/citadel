## Design (the visual canon, keep it the same in all three sprites)

- **Role:** Starter. Defender: the passive extends its defense, so the design says shield, patience and weight.
- **Concept:** a squat ember golem made of cracked grey stone and riveted iron, a round dome helmet-head with two big amber eyes, a small flame burning on top of its head.
- **Key details:** heavy riveted shoulder plates with copper trim, a glowing furnace core in the chest, thick stone fists, and a broad kite-shaped iron shield with clipped upper corners, a pointed bottom, thick copper border and cross braces, rivets, and a glowing circular ember boss with a toothed bronze surround.
- **Palette:** slate grey stone, dark iron, copper trim, orange glow.
- **Signature glow:** ember orange (amber eyes, chest furnace, flame, shield boss).
- **Main card pose:** braced low behind its big shield, shield forward on the right, one fist raised, looking at the viewer.
- **Preview framing:** head and shoulders with the top of the shield visible in front, eyes bright, flame on top.
- **Battle pose:** braced guard stance, knees bent, shield held out in front toward the right.

## How to run it

1. Read `identity.md` and inspect its primary reference. Upload the established `sprite_preview_card.webp` in the first message and add: "Redraw this exact character in the new format and keep the design."
2. Paste the main card prompt. Iterate until the design is right.
3. In the same chat paste the preview prompt, then the battle prompt, each starting with: "Use the character in the previous image as the exact reference: same design, colours, armour and proportions. "
4. Crop and convert with the commands in `../ART_GUIDE.md` section 6, check the checklist in section 7.
