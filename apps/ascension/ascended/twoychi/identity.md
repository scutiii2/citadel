# Twoychi: visual identity

Status: design brief, no sprites yet. There is no reference image. Once the first portrait is generated and approved, make it `sprite_preview_card.webp` and name it here as the primary reference, as the other Ascended do. Until then, this file and `../ART_GUIDE.md` control the design. Lore: `../../lore/README.md` (Twoychi in Unchosen and Undeserving).

## Concept

Twoychi is two beings drawn as one Ascended: **Aya**, a water-bender spirit, and **Troy**, her protector dog. Every sprite shows both. Twoychi is the spirit of death. Aya is its merciful, calm half; Troy is its guarding half. The pair must read as partners at first glance, never as a character with a pet.

Signature colours: **cyan and white** for Twoychi as a whole (Aya's colours, and the shared glow). Troy's own colours are **white and black**.

## Aya

- A water-bender spirit. Her body is a spirit: pale, softly luminous, partly translucent. Water shows through the translucent parts.
- **Her hands are translucent like a spirit's.** They are the clearest feature. Water gathers in and around the open hands and flows from them as ribbons, arcs, orbs or a stream. Show the water and the spirit hands as one design: the hands are glass-clear white with cyan glow at the palms, and the water is the same cyan and white.
- Her hair moves as if underwater even on dry ground (from the lore), in cyan-to-white strands.
- Eyes: very pale, the colour eyes go just before they close (from the lore); a cool pale cyan-white.
- Calm, merciful bearing. Never aggressive; the water is shown at rest or flowing, not as a weapon.
- Palette: white, pale cyan, deeper aqua in the water, with soft cyan light on nearby surfaces.
- **Open decision: her face.** `../ART_GUIDE.md` bans human faces. Aya is a spirit, so options are a veiled or hooded face, a face of smooth light with only the pale eyes, or a face turned away. Choose one before the first generation and record it here.

## Troy

- A shih tzu. Small, sturdy and long-coated, with a short flat face, round dark eyes, a dark nose and a feathered plume tail curled over his back. White and black coat in patches.
- He shows **all his forms**; each is the same dog scaled up and made fiercer, and each keeps the white and black coat and the shih tzu face shape:
  1. **Dog.** The small shih tzu, solemn and calm, usually resting by Aya.
  2. **Wolf.** One enemy: a lean white and black wolf with the same face markings.
  3. **Pack of six wolves.** More than one enemy: six wolves, white and black, around Aya.
  4. **Giant.** No other choice: a huge white and black beast with a shih tzu-like face, "too large for its name". The ground should look like it remembers it.
  5. **Bear** (Unchosen mentions a bear that flattens men "like a landslide"): a white and black bear, calm and massive.
- Personality changes with the form, in the style of Kindred: the dog is sleepy and watchful, the wolf focused, the pack coordinated, the giant and the bear overwhelming and indifferent. Show this in posture and expression, never in text.
- Palette: white, black, a little cool grey. Cyan appears only as rim light and eye glints from Aya's water, never as his coat.

## Together

- Aya is the focal figure. Troy stands beside or in front of her, within the same silhouette. Water curls around both of them, tying cyan to white and black.
- In the main card, Troy appears in several forms at once (small dog nearest, larger forms behind, rendered ghostly and white-black), so all forms read in one image.
- Preview: head and shoulders of Aya with her translucent hands and Troy's dog head or a wolf head. Battle: Aya and the dog as one complete figure facing right, with water arcing toward the right.
- Palette overall: cyan and white lead; black appears only on Troy. Keep the cyan-white colour family dominant.

## Consistency across sprites

Keep these in every sprite: Aya's translucent hands holding water, her pale cyan eyes, her flowing hair, Troy's white and black shih tzu markings, his curled tail and flat face, and the cyan-white water linking both. Pose, crop and background change; do not make Troy a different breed or colour, make Aya's hands opaque, or add weapons.

Battle shading follows `../ART_GUIDE.md`: darkest tones at least about #2a3342, including Troy's black, which must be a dark slate, not pure black.

## Sources

Aya and Troy come from Unchosen 2 and 3 (the water-bender and the dog who cannot be killed) and Undeserving 3, 6 and 7 (their birth). Their roles are from the story decisions in `../../lore/README.md`.
