Split the admin app into one per backend (Plan 7)

ember_admin is renamed portcullis_admin (git mv, history kept) and
trimmed to accounts, roles, invites, settings, config issues, profile
and login. New armory_admin (Capabilities, Extensions), stronghold_admin
(Usage, Analytics, Tickets, Tuning) and treasury_admin (read-only
Instances) are built from the legion_admin skeleton with their pages
copied from ember_admin. Each rail links to the other admin apps by
swapping the port; the session cookie is host-wide, so one login works
in all. New Portcullis route GET /api/admin/treasury (settings.manage)
shows Treasury's instances. docker-compose, deployment docs and
publish_live follow; ports 26500 and 26501, 26503 to 26505.

The duplicate Agents page in ember_admin was dropped (legion_admin has
it). The e2e specs for the moved pages were removed with them.

Tests: portcullis 1313 passed; vitest armory_admin 95, stronghold_admin
260, treasury_admin 32, portcullis_admin 201, legion_admin 78.
Not run: Docker builds, the apps in a browser, Playwright. legion_admin
vue-tsc still reports an old error in ConnectionsView.vue.
