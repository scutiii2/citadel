## Concept

Twoychi is two beings drawn as one Ascended: **Aya**, a water-bender spirit, and **Troy**, her protector dog. Every sprite shows both. Twoychi is the spirit of death. Aya is its merciful, calm half; Troy is its guarding half. The pair must read as partners at first glance, never as a character with a pet.

Signature colours: **cyan and white** for Twoychi as a whole (Aya's colours, and the shared glow). Troy's own colours are **white and black**.

## Aya

Aya has two appearances. Ordinary people see only the first, as the Collector saw her. The second shows only when she uses her power (healing, guiding the dead).

- **Human form (the one in front).** A young woman, calm and kind, who looks entirely human. Plain travelling clothes with a mended sleeve (from the lore), **always blue and white**: a white top or tunic with blue panels, trim and sash, blue-and-white cloth throughout, simple boots; hair dark brown, loose. Face calm and human, with pale cyan-grey eyes. She is never aggressive; water is at rest or flowing around her hands. No visible sign that she is anything more.
- **True form (the one behind).** When she uses her power, her real form shows behind her: a large, pale, softly luminous water-bender spirit, partly translucent, in cyan and white. Water shows through it. Its hands are translucent like a spirit's, glass-clear white with a cyan glow at the palms, and it controls water with them: ribbons, arcs and orbs rise from them. Its hair drifts as if underwater in cyan-to-white strands, and its eyes are very pale cyan-white (the colour eyes go just before they close, from the lore). It mirrors her pose, a little larger, and fades to mist toward the edges so it never hides her.
- Both read as one being: same pose, same flow of water. The human Aya stays in front and remains the focal figure; the true form is the glow and shape behind her.
- Palette: white, pale cyan, deeper aqua in the water, with soft cyan light on nearby surfaces.
- Her face (human form) is allowed (`../ART_GUIDE.md` no longer bans faces). Record the approved face here after the first generation.

## Troy

- A shih tzu. Small, sturdy and long-coated, with a short flat face, round dark eyes, a dark nose and a feathered plume tail curled over his back. White and black coat in patches.
- Troy has a base shape and **two forms**: **wolf** and **bear**. Show all of them. Each form has the true face and body of its animal, not a shih tzu face, but keeps Troy's white and black colour pattern (the same patch layout, the dark mask and white markings around the eyes and muzzle, and the white plume on the tail or rump) so it reads as Troy:
  1. **Dog (base).** The small shih tzu, solemn and calm, usually resting by Aya.
  2. **Wolf.** A lean white and black wolf: long muzzle, pointed ears, wolf build, Troy's colour pattern.
  3. **Bear.** A white and black bear: broad head, round ears, massive build, calm, Troy's colour pattern.
- Personality changes with the form, in the style of Kindred: the dog is sleepy and watchful, the wolf focused and fast, the bear overwhelming and indifferent. Show this in posture and expression, never in text.
- Palette: white, black, a little cool grey. Cyan appears only as rim light and eye glints from Aya's water, never as his coat.

## Together

- Human Aya is the focal figure, with her true form behind her. Troy stands beside or in front of her, within the same silhouette. Water curls around all of them, tying cyan to white and black.
- In the main card, Troy appears in all three shapes at once (small dog nearest, wolf and bear behind, rendered ghostly and white-black), so every form reads in one image.
- Preview: head and shoulders of human Aya with her true form's glowing outline behind her, and Troy's dog head. Battle: Aya and the dog as one complete figure facing right, with water arcing toward the right.
- Palette overall: cyan and white lead; black appears only on Troy. Keep the cyan-white colour family dominant.

## Consistency across sprites

Keep these in every sprite: human Aya in front with her mended sleeve and her always blue-and-white clothing, her true spirit form behind her with translucent hands controlling water, her pale cyan eyes, Troy's white and black shih tzu markings, his curled tail and flat face, and the cyan-white water linking both. Pose, crop and background change; do not change Troy's colours or patch layout, give the wolf or bear a shih tzu face, or drop the shared markings, make the true form's hands opaque, hide the true form, make the front Aya look supernatural, or put her in any clothing without blue and white, or add weapons.

Battle shading follows `../ART_GUIDE.md`: darkest tones at least about #2a3342, including Troy's black, which must be a dark slate, not pure black.
