# Twoychi: art prompts

Rules: `../ART_GUIDE.md` (read it first; it explains sizes, the black background and the crop steps).
Files for this Ascended: `sprite_main_card.webp`, `sprite_preview_card.webp`, `sprite_battle.webp` (this folder).
Status: no sprites yet. Design brief: `identity.md`. Once the first portrait is approved, save it as `sprite_preview_card.webp` and name it the primary reference in `identity.md`.

## How to run it

1. Read `identity.md`. Start a new chat. Paste the main card prompt and iterate until Aya, Troy and his two forms are right.
2. In the same chat paste the preview prompt, then the battle prompt, each starting with: "Use the character in the previous image as the exact reference: same design, colours and proportions. "
3. Crop and convert with the commands in `../ART_GUIDE.md` section 6 and check section 7.

## Main card prompt (`sprite_main_card.webp`)

```text
Create the card illustration of an original game creature. Twoychi is one game creature made of two beings, Aya and Troy, always shown together. Aya is a calm, merciful water-bender spirit: her body is pale, softly luminous and partly translucent; her hands are translucent like a spirit's, glass-clear white with a cyan glow at the palms, and water gathers in and flows from them as cyan-and-white ribbons and orbs; her hair drifts as if underwater in cyan-to-white strands; her eyes are very pale cyan-white; her face is calm, pale and spirit-like. Troy is her protector dog, a white-and-black shih tzu: small, sturdy, long-coated, with a short flat face, round dark eyes, a dark nose and a feathered tail curled over his back, in white and black patches. Troy also has two larger forms, a wolf and a bear, which keep the same white-and-black coat and the same flat shih tzu face shape. Pose: Aya floats low in the centre with both translucent hands open and water flowing between them; Troy the small shih tzu sits close in front of her, calm; behind them, ghostly and half-transparent, the wolf form and the bear form rise on either side, so all three forms of Troy read in one image. Water curls around everyone, tying cyan and white to Troy's black and white. Format: landscape 3:2 (1536 x 1024). Composition: the whole group visible and centred, filling about 85 percent of the height of the middle band, where the middle band is the middle 66 percent of the image height. Keep the top 17 percent and the bottom 17 percent as plain background only, with nothing important there, because the picture will be cropped to a wide 1.93:1 strip. No limb, water stream or effect touches the left or right edge. Background: a dark ruined shrine or dungeon with a faint distant glow, soft and darker toward the edges so the characters stand out, never brighter than the characters. Style: Chunky high-resolution pixel art in a dark-fantasy style: visible square pixels about 4 to 6 real pixels wide, crisp hard edges, no smooth gradients, no anti-aliasing blur, not painterly, not 3D, not photographic. Palette: cyan and white lead (water, spirit glow, Aya), with white-and-black fur on Troy, a little cool slate, and the shared ember-orange only as faint distant forge glow in the background. One strong light source that comes from the character's own cyan glow. A clear silhouette, calm and a little fierce. Avoid pure white and pure black. Never: No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, signature, speech bubbles, UI, card frame or border. No other characters besides Aya and Troy (they are one Ascended). No ordinary humans, no blood.
```
