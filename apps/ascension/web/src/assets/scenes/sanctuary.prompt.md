# Sanctuary background

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-10-14, using the approved collection mockup as a style reference. Converted to WebP at quality 90 for the application.

Prompt:

Generate a BACKGROUND ASSET ONLY for the Ascension game based closely on the environment in this approved UI reference. No UI, no text, no logos, no cards, no characters, no borders. Wide landscape 16:9 high resolution pixel-art ruined sanctuary: towering blue-black stone arched pillars at both sides, central distant staircase receding into cyan mist, dim cyan pools reflected on stone floor at bottom, sparse very small warm torchlights in distance. Match exactly the quiet deep navy/cyan environment of reference. Dark low contrast but visible masonry, dark open central area for overlay content. Keep most architectural detail toward sides and floor, subtle depth, restrained light, no bright white sky, no orange foreground torches. Crisp detailed pixel art, no blur, not photorealistic. This will be a continuous shared background behind collection, shop, and battle UI, with transparent navy surfaces on top.
