# Bruiser visual identity

## Approved redesign (2026-10-13)

The user approved the character artwork in the collection and shop concepts and explicitly requested this design for the battle sprite. This intentional redesign supersedes the former creature design for newly generated art.

A broad stocky fantasy dwarf-like heavy fighter with a stern face, dark full beard, short dark hair, bulky slate-steel armour with bronze rivets, massive spiked shoulder plates, thick gauntlets and boots, dark brown clothing, and a large block-headed steel warhammer. Match the approved armoured hammer fighter; square squat silhouette, visibly heavier than Guardian. No boar snout, tusks or molten belly.

## References and consistency

- Primary new-design reference: `battle_source.png`, the generated full-resolution source for `sprite_battle.webp` in this folder.
- `sprite_battle.webp` is the 512 x 512 game export of that source.
- The approved UI concept sheets supplied the original design direction; the character-only source is the reference for future poses and card artwork.
- Existing `sprite_main_card.webp` and `sprite_preview_card.webp` retain the previous design because this request covers battle sprites only. Do not use those legacy cards to override the approved redesign. Updating the cards is a separate art pass.
- Preserve face/helmet, silhouette, clothing, armour, weapons, palette and proportions across future sprites. Correct accidental drift rather than changing this identity.

## Battle framing

Right-facing three-quarter heavy combat stance, wide planted feet and hammer gripped in both hands, hammer head held ready forward/right. A single full-body figure on transparent background, with no ground, cast shadow, text or frame. Nothing touches an edge. Use crisp square pixel clusters and dark slate shadows approximately #2a3342 or lighter so the arena's lighten blend does not erase details. Export at 512 x 512, lossless WebP, with nearest-neighbour scaling.
