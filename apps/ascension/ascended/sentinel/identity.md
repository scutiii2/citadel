# Sentinel visual identity

Primary reference: `sprite_preview_card.webp`, the established portrait. Preserve this reference when recreating the main card and battle sprites. Existing main and battle files initially duplicate the portrait and are not framing references.

## Defining features

- An ancient, watchful armoured owl defender; entirely a creature, with no human face.
- Broad owl head, pale warm-grey facial feather discs, a short dark hooked beak, large angular violet glowing eyes, and two tall swept-up feather tufts with pale gold edges.
- Stocky, sturdy armoured body with a large head, broad shoulders, thick gauntlets and taloned armoured feet. Keep the owl anatomy and proportions consistent across views.
- Cracked charcoal/slate stone and metal plates, muted antique gold edging and rivets. Battle shading must stay at or above dark slate, approximately #2a3342, for the arena blend.
- Layered dark purple feather shoulder mantle and long hanging feather cloak, with jagged pointed feather tips.
- A large violet diamond chest gem in a pointed gold setting; smaller violet diamond gems at the belt and sword hilt.
- Tall pointed kite shield, held on the viewer's right in the main card: dark slate face, thick gold rim and rivets, central violet diamond in an elongated gold star-shaped setting.
- Sheathed sword at the opposite hip, with a gold-edged hilt and violet pommel detail. Do not add a new weapon design.
- Violet eyes and gems provide the signature light. Ember orange belongs mainly to the distant forge scenery. Preserve charcoal, purple and antique gold as the dominant palette.

## Framing and pose

- Main: upright full-body hero guard pose, looking toward the viewer, shield forward on the viewer's right. Whole figure and equipment fit within the safe central band of the landscape source.
- Battle: full body in a right-facing three-quarter guard stance, shield forward toward the right, mantle behind. Flat pure black background (or clean transparency), no floor or cast shadow, clear margins.
- Preview: keep the established image as the primary design reference; its current portrait framing is not a template for the new output dimensions.

Pose, crop and background changes must not alter the face, feather tufts, gems, armour ornament, shield silhouette or proportions. Correct accidental generation drift rather than updating this identity to match it.

## Preview export reference

`sprite_preview_card.webp` is the 512 x 512 game export and the primary reference. The export correction changes framing and dimensions only, not the character identity.
